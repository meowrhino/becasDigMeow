and if you'd rather not, we'll maintain it for you

methodology

  1. 01

    we start with a free meeting. bring 3–5 references of things you like or don't: websites, other interfaces, sketches on a napkin. we go through them together and talk about structure, look and money. before we leave we set a date for the second one and a list of what we need from you: texts, images and your visual identity if you have one.

  2. 02

    your website is ready in a month, counting from the day your material lands — not from the day we meet. we hold the second meeting within the two weeks after the first, with your material already sent. if more than two weeks go by and there is no material, we stop — we have set aside a month of our calendar and we cannot keep it hanging. picking it up again costs 10%, which is what it costs to make room for you again. it is not a fine: the month we were keeping for you already went to someone else.

  3. 03

    with the material on the table we hand the process back to you: how we have understood your project and a design proposal. two weeks after it lands, at the latest. take as long as you need to decide: better you think it over now than halfway through the coding. once you are happy and the first payment lands (half), we start writing code.

  4. 04

    there are three deliveries —the design, the code once it starts to take shape, and the final code— and one round of revisions per delivery. each round can hold several changes. what we close at one delivery does not reopen at the next: it is not rigidity, reopening closed decisions is exactly what turns one month into six. the design does move again when it goes from sketch to a real website that has to work on a phone: that does not count as a round, it is part of the job. design usually takes 1–2 weeks and coding another 1–2. with the material on time, a month end to end.

  5. 05

    with the code approved and the other half paid, we meet one last time, in front of your computer. you leave that meeting knowing how to update your website without us: that is the point, not an extra. if you need it we set up your domain (we recommend cloudflare) and it stays in your name, not ours.

  6. 06

    after delivery you have two weeks to tell us what is broken or to spend the rounds you have left. from there, changes are €60/hour. we say it plainly because the alternative is the usual one: a monthly fee you pay every month for nothing. here you only pay when you actually want to change something — and if you learned to do it yourself, you do not pay.
