metodología

  1. 01

    we start with a free first meeting. bring 3–5 references of things you like or don't like: they can be websites, other interfaces, or even sketches. we go through the material together and talk about structure, aesthetics, and budget. before we say goodbye, we schedule the second meeting and a list of what we need from you: texts, images and your visual identity.

  2. 02

    your website is ready in a month, counting from the first meeting. the second meeting is included as long as we hold it within the next two weeks and you've sent your material. if more than two weeks pass without content, the project is paused and restarting it carries a fee of 10% of the agreed price.

  3. 03

    from there, we put together a design proposal. we give you time to think it over. once you're happy with it and the first payment is made (50%), we start coding.

  4. 04

    throughout the project you have up to 3 rounds of revisions. each round can include several adjustments. the design also adapts as it goes from mockup to a working website that looks good on different devices. we usually take 1–2 weeks for design and 1–2 weeks for development: with your material on time, a month from start to finish.

  5. 05

    once the code is approved and the second payment is made (50%), we meet one last time to set up your device: you'll learn to update the website yourself. if you need it, we also configure your domain (we recommend cloudflare).

  6. 06

    after delivery you have 2 weeks to report bugs or use any remaining revision rounds. after that period, changes are billed at 60€/hour.
