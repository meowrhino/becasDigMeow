project · a website for fashion and editorial photography

andrea carilla

andrea carilla is a photographer and she moves between very different registers: editorial, fashion story, look book, product, artist image. her website is a gallery with a hazy menu: every time you come in it's a different page. there's no fixed order and no star photo, because none of her photos is the cover of the others — you're the one who decides what to look at first, and no two visits see the same thing. and hers was the very first: the first website that came out of meowrhino studio. thank you andrea, t'estimo. we wanted the portfolio to be truly hers, from the structure down to the last visual detail — built from scratch, no frameworks and no site builders, and andrea learned to update it herself in the project's last meeting. and soon it'll also be the studio's first online shop.
