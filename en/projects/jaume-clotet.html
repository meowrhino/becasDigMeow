project · a world-website for jaume clotet

jaume clotet

jaume has projects with names like alusinasons, cantautot or vampirtistes — a universe of his own that called for a world-website, not a portfolio of boxes. every project has its own space and its own navigation, and the whole thing is travelled more like you explore a record than like you read a cv. jaume chose six projects, not one more, and the website is built so that all six get walked through: when you've seen them all, a ghost appears. nothing announces it — either you go through the whole thing or you never find out it's there. that doesn't come out of any site builder; it comes out of a conversation with jaume.
