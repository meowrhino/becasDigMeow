project · a website for analogue film and performance

barbara wong

bárbara works with analogue film, video installation, performance and writing from an ecofeminist perspective. her website brings together work, news and a diary — three different publishing rhythms under one roof. we built it so she can feed it without touching code: she edits a few text files and the website reorganises itself. it's many years of work, and with that much material the temptation is to cram it all in. we did the opposite: a very clean website, with air, so each piece can be seen on its own.
