project · a website for game audio

noan cittadino

noan composes music and designs sound for video games: dynamic music systems, fmod, wwise. his portfolio had to speak two languages at once — that of game studios looking for a technical profile, and that of anyone who just wants to hear his work. we solved it through the simple and the tender: a small desktop where everything you touch makes a sound, with little sounds made by noan himself. the portfolio of a sound designer that sounds, instead of telling you that it sounds.
