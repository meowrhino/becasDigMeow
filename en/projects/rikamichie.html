project · a website for a modern troubadour

rikamichie

erika michi is a modern troubadour: musician, director and masseuse, raised on the slopes of montjuïc. her website had to sound like her — not like one more artist template. we built it from scratch, thinking through every section with her: her music, her projects and her way of telling things all fit in a single website she can update herself, without paying subscriptions or depending on anyone. the navigation runs crosswise: each side of her in one direction, and erika in the middle. because it was the only way to keep any one of her sides from ending up beneath the others.
