el mundo de las jordis

el mundo de las jordis is a diary written by several hands: a little window into their world. from the outside it has what a social network has — timeline, hashtags, the places where things happen — and on the inside it has none of what makes them unbearable: no algorithm deciding what you see, no ads, no numbers telling you whether what you wrote is worth anything. several people write in it and nobody is putting it in order. technically, the engine comes from twoitter, an earlier experiment of ours — we like it when the pieces of one project find a second life in another.
