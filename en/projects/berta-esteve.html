berta esteve

berta is a curator, researcher and artist, and her bio says "expos, jajas & internet" — with that brief, a serious cv website was off the table. we made a website that takes her work seriously without taking itself seriously, in catalan and on a .cat domain. underneath it's the most complex thing we've built: berta produces an enormous amount, and all of it had to fit without the navigation breaking or the whole thing looking like a dead archive.
