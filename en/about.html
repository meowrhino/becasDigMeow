shall i help you make your little corner of the internet?

we are a web design studio in barcelona.

we make websites for a more communal, close and living internet.

websites from scratch, with you: unique inside and out.

we teach you to maintain and customise them your way.

and if you'd rather not do it yourself, we maintain them for you.

who we are

a freelancer who makes websites for his friends.

behind meowrhino studio there is manu latour. we design and code from barcelona, we come from art and design —escola massana— and since 2020 we've made custom websites for artists, photographers, musicians, collectives and people working for themselves.

we also make small free tools for whatever we need: compressing images without uploading them to any server, generating invoices without signing up, working out what you actually earn as a freelancer.

why

the internet can be something else.

it matters that we start imagining a future outside social media. there are a thousand reasons: censorship. protest against technocrats. being able to delete instagram when you've had enough of the news, without disappearing off the map. your work not being an excuse to keep scrolling. having a place where things can work differently.

social media is turning more and more into content platforms. you publish in the format you're given, for whichever algorithm is in charge, and what you make never quite ends up being yours.

a website of your own is a different thing. it's a space where you decide what gets told about you, how it's told and how it looks. some people call these digital gardens: small spaces that don't have to look like the places where we spend the day.

we think we're still in time to use the internet differently.

and besides, it's yours. the day you fall out with the server, with the provider or with us, you take your files and go. there is nothing to rescue, because it never stopped being yours.

an internet of your own

a social network is a space someone else built for you. a website is a space you build yourself.

you decide where everything goes, how the elements are laid out, what you upload and what you don't. you don't have to fit any platform's format.

if your portfolio makes sense as a floppy drive, it is a floppy drive.

the internet is far too big for all of us to have the same page.

how we work

we make websites from scratch, with you: you're part of the creative process and we make the decisions together.

a project takes a month end to end and starts when you send us your material. three deliveries, one round of revisions after each, and you leave knowing how to maintain your website without us.
