shall i help you make your little corner of the internet?

we are a small web design and development studio in barcelona.

we make websites for a more communal, close and living internet.

websites from scratch for artists, photographers, musicians, collectives and people who work for themselves.

we don't make websites for you to have and forget about. we make websites for you to keep.

who's behind this

behind meowrhino it's me: manu latour. i studied at la massana and the uab, and since 2020 i have been designing and coding custom websites from barcelona.

i work with you through the whole process: we think out the structure together, i design the interface and i write the code. there are no templates — your project doesn't have to fit inside a website that already exists.

i also make free tools for whatever i happen to need: compressing images without uploading them to any server, generating invoices without signing up, working out what you really earn as a freelancer.

the internet can be something else

we like the internet when it still looks like a place where things can happen.

we spend more and more time on platforms that decide how you publish, what you are allowed to show and who gets to see it.

a website of your own works differently: you decide what you tell, how you tell it and how it looks. you don't have to fit anyone's format or design your work for an algorithm.

your website can be simple, strange, experimental, tiny or completely absurd. if your portfolio makes sense as a floppy drive, we make a floppy drive.

the internet is far too big for all of us to have the same page.

your website is yours

when we are done you take the code and the files with you.

we teach you to maintain it and change it on your own. if you would rather we did it, we are still here.

we don't want you to depend on us to understand what you have.

the day you fall out with the server, with the provider or with us, you pick up your files and take them with you. there is nothing to rescue, because it never stopped being yours.

if you have an idea, even a half-formed one, write to us and we'll talk it through.
