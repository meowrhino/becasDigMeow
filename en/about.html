shall i make you a website?

we are a small web design and development studio in barcelona.

we make websites from scratch for people who want a place of their own on the internet, with no templates and no unnecessary maintenance.

websites that can be simple, strange, experimental or completely absurd, and that you can update on your own.

the internet can be something else

the internet is not instagram, and it is not google.

the internet is also all those small pages that someone made because they wanted to tell, teach or share something. but more and more, like so many things, it is becoming the property of a few.

and it is worth having a place of your own, one that looks the way you want and that somebody actually made.

we make every website from scratch so that you decide what you want to tell and how you want to tell it. we come up with the concept, think out the structure, design the interface and write the code.

your website is yours

the repository on github is yours and the domain on cloudflare is yours. there will be no ads and no template forcing anything on your texts, your images or your projects.

the code we write is yours to change, or anyone else's you choose, and github keeps the history of everything you have been doing.

a website can be far simpler than it looks: text, images and code. we use a structure that lets you update the content without having to know how to program.

hosting can be free and the domain is pretty much the only expense that comes back.

and if one day you want to change hosting, developer or provider, you can. we always recommend working with local providers.

i keep access to your website in case you ever need help, but you don't have to keep working with me for it to keep running, and you can revoke my access whenever you want. it is there so i can give you a hand if something ever happens, or so you can ask me for the updates you need.

if you want me to make the changes for you, you can hire me by the hour. if you want to do it yourself, we'll show you how.

who's behind this

behind meowrhino it's me: manu latour.

i studied at la massana and the uab, and since 2020 i have been designing and coding custom websites from barcelona.

the internet has been with me for a long time and it has kept me good company: i think of my work as making the internet a slightly better place with every project we build.

i want to understand what you do, what you need and what can work best for you.

i want you to end up with a website you show off, one that builds whatever story you need: professional photographer, multinational company, internet microcelebrity.

and i give you the tools you need to look after it: imgToWeb to compress images, colorFun to pick colours…

digital gardens

some people call this digital gardens: small spaces of the internet that are cultivated instead of published and abandoned.

they grow slowly. they change shape. they have half-finished things and things that have been there for years.

they don't need to compete for our attention.

they are simply there.

we like to think your website can be one of those.

a small place that exists because you decided it should exist.
