shall i help you make your little corner of the internet?

we are a web design studio in barcelona.

we make websites for a more communal, close and living internet.

websites from scratch, with you: unique inside and out.

we teach you to maintain and customise them your way, so you don't depend on us every time you want to change something.

the internet is full of platforms that decide how things are shown to you, of templates that make everything look alike, and of social networks that can change the rules whenever they please.

we believe a website of your own can be something else: a space that is yours, made to your measure, that you can look after and change over time.

that's why we design and code every website from scratch. we talk with you, we understand what you need and we build something that makes sense for you, not for a template.

we don't make websites for you to have and forget about. we make websites for you to keep.

behind meowrhino studio there's me, manu. i design and code websites from barcelona and i work with artists, photographers, musicians, cultural projects, small businesses and people who want a place of their own on the internet.

if you have an idea, even a half-formed one, we can talk it through.

a website from scratch costs 850 € + VAT.

it includes the design, the development, three rounds of revisions and a final session with you to teach you to maintain it on your own.

if you need something different, write to me and we'll talk.
