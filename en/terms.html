the small print

work

what we do

we design and develop custom websites. they are static websites: files, no server. the content lives in a file you learn to read and edit: texts, images and files, without depending on anyone.

the code is yours

you can use it, transform it, continue it or give it to whoever you want. we work in our own repository and, on delivery, we hand you a full copy in your github account, in your name.

we keep the original with its whole history: it is the record of our work and what lets us keep showing the project even if one day your website goes offline. that copy of ours stays published, without a domain, at a meowrhino address, so we can link to it if one day the domain expires or anything happens to your website. we also stay on as administrators of your copy, so we can give you a hand if you need it; you can remove that access whenever you want.

the material

in the first meeting we set the date on which you send us the material: texts, images and a minimal visual identity (logo, colour palette, typography). if you don't have one, it can be quoted as part of the project. the texts come in every language the website will have: translations are part of the material.

until that date we work on a sketch; the final design is made with your real material, not with placeholders, and the month of work starts the day it arrives.

deliveries and rounds

each project includes 3 deliveries · 1 round of changes per delivery: design, mid-stage development and final development. each round may include several changes, but once it is sent, the next ones belong to the following round. what is approved in one delivery is not reopened in the next.

extra rounds are charged by the hour.

payment

in two parts: one when the project is confirmed —it reserves the month in our calendar and lets us start— and one before the final delivery, with the website finished and before it goes live. you choose the split, between 40 and 60 % each.

domain and hosting

you buy the domain and it stays in your name, not ours. we help you choose it and set it up —we recommend cloudflare—, but ownership and the yearly renewal are yours. if it expires, the website stops being visible; the files are still yours and can be published again whenever you want.

the website is served as static files, with no server of its own and no database, from a cloudflare —or github— account that is also yours and whose free plan more than covers a project of this size. hosting has no monthly cost: the domain is the only recurring expense. if the project needs something more —a shop, for example—, it is quoted separately and also lives in your accounts.

after delivery

you have two weeks of warranty: if anything we delivered breaks, we fix it. after that —or if the problem comes from a change you made while editing the website— the fix is charged by the hour, like any new change.

deadlines and cancellation

the first meeting is free, and so is the second one if it comes with your material on the date we set.

if, once the project is confirmed, it sits still for more than two weeks waiting for material, picking it up again costs 10 % of the budget: we had set aside the month and we have to make room for you again.

you can stop the project at any time by paying the proportional part of the work done; if it is picked up later, a new budget is opened.

portfolio

we reserve the right to publish the project in our portfolio and social media. once the website is finished, we propose a joint post on instagram.

our commitment

to design a website you understand, that you can maintain and that can grow with you.
