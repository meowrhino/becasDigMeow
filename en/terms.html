terms

we design and develop custom websites using jamstack technology. your website data is managed through a file you learn to read and edit: you can add, remove and change texts, images and files without depending on anyone.

the code is yours. you can use it, transform it, continue it or give it to whoever you want. meowrhino.studio keeps a copy of each project as a record of authorship and to ensure the code remains free and accessible.

we do not start a project without all the material ready: texts, images and content. also a minimal visual identity (logo, colour palette, typography). if you don't have one, it can be quoted as part of the project.

we don't work with placeholders. the working month starts the day your material lands.

each project includes 3 deliveries (design, mid-stage development and final development) and one round of revisions per delivery. each round may include several changes, but once submitted, subsequent changes belong to the next round. what is approved at one delivery is not reopened at the next. any change resulting from working with incomplete material counts as a revision. extra rounds are billed at 60€/hour.

payment in two phases: 50% upon design sign-off, 50% upon completing development.

the first meeting is free, and so is the second as long as we hold it within the next two weeks and your material is ready. after that period without content, the project is paused and restarting it carries a fee of 10% of the budget. from the first delivery onwards, part of the budget is used. you can stop the project at any time by paying the proportional share of the work done; if resumed later, a new quote is provided.

we reserve the option to publish the project in our portfolio and social media. upon completion of the website, we'll suggest a joint instagram post.

our commitment is to design a website you understand, can maintain, and that can grow with you.

accepting the quote means accepting these conditions.
