an online shop for zines

quien no corre, vuela

quien no corre, vuela is a self-publishing project: photography zines that embrace error and make room for the archive, the intimate and the everyday. their shop is custom-built for them. each zine has its own page with the details of the edition: author, print run, binding, pages and size. authors have their own pages too, and there's a calendar of fairs and launches. you pay by card, and behind the scenes there's a panel for stock, orders with their tracking number, and the newsletter. their calligraphic logo sits at the top, and titles are set in garamond italic. in spanish, catalan and english.
