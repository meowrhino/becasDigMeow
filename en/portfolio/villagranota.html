an experimental website with a frog

villagranota

the website's title is a frog emoji, and that already tells you the spirit of the project. villagranota lives at frogciti.es and it's about one simple idea: getting html out of technical places and into everyday ones, so that anyone can play with it. it sums itself up in three words — honesty, expressing yourself for real on your own website; playfulness, making it without solemnity; and rawness, because plain html and css are already beautiful. the whole site weighs six kilobytes and its text scrolls sideways in a marquee, a tag that's been obsolete for twenty years: the joke is the message. projects like this are our laboratory, and what we learn making odd websites ends up improving the "serious" ones.
