a website for scenography and stage direction

pol roig

pol roig valldosera (barcelona, 2000) is a set designer and stage director, trained at the institut del teatre. he has shown his work at the liceu, sala beckett, l'auditori, mercat de les flors and festival grec. his website opens with a full-screen stage photo and a procession of little figures walking in along a dotted line. then comes a single black page with his own works and his scenography, and between projects, more figures: some are characters who have escaped from one of his pieces, and clicking them takes you to it. each project has its own page, in white. in catalan and english.
