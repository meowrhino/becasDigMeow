a map-website for a textile artist

almudena gonzalez

almudena gonzález is a visual artist and researcher working between photography, material research and digital jacquard weaving. her home page is a map you can drag around, with the photos of all her projects, and the ones from the same project are joined by a fine thread in its colour. hover over a photo and its project lights up, while its threads come unstitched and sew themselves back along another path. each project has its own colour, plus a neon one for dark mode. the work is split into textile art and editorial, and the diaries are a page of loose photos that come up in a different order on every visit, because that's how she asked for it. in spanish, english and catalan.
