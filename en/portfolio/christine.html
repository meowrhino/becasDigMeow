a portfolio that rearranges itself around your question

christine

christine janjira meyer is a graphic designer. her website has no menu: it has two axes. you pick which two you want to look at her work through — is this illustration or graphic design? is it artwork or commercial? is it demure or opinionated? is it silly or serious? — and the pieces rearrange themselves on the map. every visit opens with a different pair, picked at random, so you never start from the same question. it's a designer sorting her own work by the tensions that actually matter to her, and letting you choose the criteria. no template does that, because no template knows what matters to you.
