a website for a commercial and music video director

la diegol

la diegol is marta diego, a director born and raised in barcelona. she makes commercials and music videos, and her clients include loewe, samsung, cupra and fc barcelona. she comes from dance and the performing arts, and movement is central to how she builds a scene. the website is black: you enter through a run of her stills, full screen, with her name on top, and below is the grid of work, with a filter that splits commercials from music videos. each cover is the thumbnail the video already has on vimeo, so there's no cover to prepare separately. her name is set in wild trave and everything else in an uppercase mono.
