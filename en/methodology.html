methodology

  1. 01

    we talk

    the first meeting is free and commits you to nothing. we start by understanding what you do, what you need and what you would like your website to be.

    bring 3–5 references of things you like or don't: websites, posters, video games, interfaces you've come across anywhere. we go through them together and talk about structure, look and money.

    we set a date for the next one and a list of what we need from you: texts, images and your visual identity if you have one.

  2. 02

    we think

    with the material in front of us we give the project its shape: structure, contents, look and behaviour.

    we design with you, not for you.

  3. 03

    we design

    in a week or two you have a first proposal. we go through it together and keep refining it until it makes sense.

    take as long as you need to decide. it is much easier to change direction here than once we are already coding.

  4. 04

    we build

    with the design approved we start writing code and the website begins to exist for real.

    the design will evolve as we adapt it to phones and other possible screens: it has to work, and whatever comes up while building it has to be solved. that is part of the job, not a round of revisions.

    this usually takes another week or two. design and code back to back, and there is your month.

  5. 05

    we hand it over

    we meet one last time, in front of your computer. you leave that meeting knowing how to update your website without us: that is the point, not an extra.

    we set up the domain: it is best if you wait and buy it with us —we recommend cloudflare— and it always stays in your name.

  6. 06

    and after that

    the code is yours. you can carry on by yourself or ask us for help whenever you need it.

    you don't need to pay us a monthly fee to keep a website running.

a few things worth knowing

the project takes roughly a month from the day we receive all the material we need. we don't work with placeholders.

3 deliveries · 1 round of revisions per delivery. each round can hold several changes; once it is sent, whatever comes next goes to the following one.

50% when the project is confirmed · 50% before the final delivery.

the first payment reserves the project and lets us start working. the second one happens once the website is finished and before it goes live.

2 weeks of warranty after delivery. during that time you can tell us about anything that breaks.

after that, 60 €/hour for changes. and if you learned to do it yourself, you don't pay.

if the project sits still for more than two weeks for lack of material, it restarts with a 10%. we had set aside a month of our calendar and we have to make room for you again.
