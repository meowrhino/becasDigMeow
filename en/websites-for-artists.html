websites for artists in barcelona

almost every website we have made is for people who make things: musicians, painters, photographers, illustrators, sound designers, performers. it is what we like doing and what we do best.

an artist's website is not a catalogue: it is where your work looks the way you want, with no algorithm deciding who sees it and no template making it look like everyone else's.

no artist template

we start by understanding your work. erika michi's website navigates in a cross because her sides could not sit one below the other; mike's is a cd rack because he designs record sleeves; la diega's makes sound. every decision comes from what you do, not from a downloaded theme.

  • rikamichie a website for a modern troubadour
  • mike bros a portfolio that is a cd rack

what an artist's website usually needs

a player that does not stop when you change page, like diego san marcos's. a gig or exhibition calendar you update yourself in two minutes. a private archive to show work without publishing it, like conor's. and one url per language, in spanish, catalan and english.

  • diego san marcos a website for music and immersive audio
  • conor a photography website with a private archive
