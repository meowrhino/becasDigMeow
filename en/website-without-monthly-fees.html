a website without monthly fees

many of the websites people pay for today are rented: a monthly fee for the builder, another for the hosting, another for maintenance. the day you stop paying, the website disappears. and it is not yours: you cannot take it with you, open it up, or hand it to someone else.

ours work the other way round. you pay once for the work of designing and coding it, and the website is yours: the code, the domain and the account it is published from.

why there is no fee

because there is nothing to charge for each month. the website is served as static files, with no server of its own and no database, from a cloudflare or github account that is also yours and whose free plan more than covers a project this size. there is no server to maintain, no plugins to update, no database that can go down.

the only thing you renew: the domain

you buy the domain and it stays in your name, not ours. we help you choose it and set it up, but the ownership and the yearly renewal are yours. if it ever expires, the website stops showing, but the files are still yours and can be published again whenever you like.

the code is yours

we work in our repository and, on delivery, we hand you a full copy in your own github account, in your name. you can use it, transform it, continue it or give it to whoever you like. we stay on as administrators of your copy so we can help if you need it, and you can remove that access whenever you want.

you update it

we hand it over in person and you leave knowing how to change texts, images and files: the content lives in a file you learn to read and edit. you do not need to pay us to change a date or upload a photo.

and if something breaks

you have two weeks of guarantee after delivery: if anything we made fails, we fix it. after that, new changes are billed by the hour, like any job, and only when you ask for them. no compulsory maintenance. the full small print is in the terms →

what does cost extra

if your project needs something that is not static —a shop, bookings, user accounts— it is quoted separately and still goes in your own accounts. we tell you in the first meeting, which is free, before you decide anything.

who it makes sense for

for anyone who wants a website that lasts: people who make music, art or photography, and studios, associations and small businesses that do not want yet another subscription. if you are an artist, here is how we make those →
