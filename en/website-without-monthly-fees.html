a website without monthly fees

many websites are rented: one fee for the builder, another for the hosting, another for maintenance. the day you stop paying, the website disappears. and it is not yours.

ours work the other way round: you pay once for the design and the code, and the website is yours. the code, the domain and the account it is published from.

why there is no fee

because there is nothing to charge for each month. the website is static files, with no server and no database, published from a cloudflare or github account in your name whose free plan more than covers a project like this. nothing to maintain, nothing that can go down.

the only thing you renew: the domain

you buy it and it stays in your name; we help you choose it and set it up. if it ever expires, the website stops showing, but the files are still yours and can be published again.

the code is yours and you update it

on delivery we hand you a full copy in your own github account, and you leave the last meeting knowing how to change texts, images and files. you do not need to pay us to change a date or upload a photo. what the handover looks like →

and if something breaks

two weeks of guarantee; after that, changes are billed by the hour and only when you ask for them. no compulsory maintenance. if the project needs something that is not static —a shop, bookings— it is quoted separately and still goes in your own accounts. the small print →

who it makes sense for

for anyone who wants a website that lasts: artists, studios, associations and small businesses like a tattoo studio or a make-up artist, who do not want yet another subscription.
