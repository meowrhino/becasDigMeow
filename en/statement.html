the internet can be something else

the internet is not instagram, and it is not google.

the internet is also all those small pages that someone made because they wanted to tell, teach or share something. but more and more, like so many things, it is becoming the property of a few.

and it is worth having a space of your own, one that looks the way you want and that somebody actually made, following your wishes.

that is why we design every website with you, not for you. you decide what you want to tell and how you want to tell it; we come up with the concept, think out the structure, draw the interface and write the code. it is not handing you the work: it is the only way your corner of the internet is yours, and still yours in five years.

why we do this

the internet has been with us for a long time and it has kept us good company. making the internet a slightly better place is the whole point of our work, of every project we build.

we want to understand what you do, what you need and what can work best for you: a photographer, a small business, someone who wants to tell a story and put it within reach of whoever wants to know it.

we want you to end up with a website you show off.

your website is yours

the repository on github is yours and the domain on cloudflare is yours. there will be no ads and no templates forcing anything on you: not in your texts, not in your images, not in your projects.

the code we write is yours to change, or anyone else's you choose, and github keeps the history of everything you have been doing.

a website can be far simpler than it looks: text, images and code. we use a structure that lets you update the content without having to know how to program, and we show you how.

and if one day you want to change hosting, studio or provider, you can: you don't have to keep working with us for your website to keep running.

digital gardens

some people call this digital gardens: small spaces of the internet that are cultivated instead of published and abandoned.

they grow slowly. they change shape. they have half-finished things and things that have been there for years.

they don't need to compete for our attention, they are simply there. we like to think your website can be one of those, a small place that exists because you decided it should exist.
